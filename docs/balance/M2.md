# Balance report: M2 (Sum layer)

M2 has no knobs: every M2 constant is frozen (GDD §14.3). This report records the bot's measured
pacing against the M2 blocking bands of GDD §15 (`balance/targets.json`) and against the
prototype numbers of §5.5 and §15 (`docs/prototypes/sim2.py`, `sim4.py`, `idle_sim.py`).

## Bot

`src/sim/bot.ts` drives the engine only through actions and exact 1 s steps (`step`):

- **Policy** (the `sim2.py` rule): buy the highest affordable tier, searching G8 down to G1;
  buy a global level instead when it is affordable and either no tier is affordable or it costs
  less than 3× that tier's purchase. Repeat until nothing is affordable.
- **Seeds** vary a 0–2 s reaction delay after each round with purchases. The policy has no
  exact cost ties to break (a global level costs 10^(2+L), and 3× a tier price is never a power
  of ten), so the tier choice itself does not depend on the seed.
- **Profiles:** `active` may act every second; `idle` during the first 10 s of every 15 min,
  starting at t = 0.
- **Events:** `G<k>` is the tier's first purchase; `2^128` and `1e<10n>` are the first times x
  reaches them, checked after each 1 s step.

Reproduce: `npm run sim -- --profile active --minutes 30 --seed <n>` and
`npm run sim -- --profile idle --minutes 120 --seed <n>` (output in `sim-output/`).

**Longest gap between meaningful events** (reported, not blocking in M2): the report counts the
stretch after the last event up to the end of the run. In the 30 min active run (seed 1) the
last tier unlock is G8 at 10.70 min, so the longest gap is the trailing 19.30 min; the longest
gap between two unlocks is 2.05 min. That stall is where M5's Product reset takes over; the
blocking gap bands start at M13 (§15).

## Prototype check (§5.5)

At 1e40 (12.03 min, active seed 1) the counts (b1…b8) are (128, 95, 72, 55, 42, 30, 20, 11) at
global level 38; the prototype has (130, 95, 72, 55, 42, 30, 20, 11) at 12.2 min. 1e100 arrives
at 22.1–22.2 min (prototype 22.6). The small differences come from the exact integrator (the
prototypes use Euler steps of 0.25–0.5 s) and the reaction delays.

## Cost

A flushing 50 ms tick on a late-M2 state (all eight tiers owned) makes 146 `Num` operations
(budget 400, GDD §21.7; `npm run bench`). Each bot run above (30 min active, 3 h idle) takes
well under a second of wall time, so `tests/sim/early.test.ts` runs in `npm run check`.
